## 通用要点

| | 值 |
|---|---|
| 尺寸 | 1920×1080 或 2560×1440（**横幅**） |
| 呈现 | `objectFit: cover` 全屏铺满并裁掉溢出 |
| 留白 | **底部居中**要留干净——文案框在 `bottom:48px` 居中，宽 min(560px,86vw)；有 vignette 压暗辅助可读 |
| 主体位置 | **远离四边**，cover 在窄屏上会左右裁掉不少 |

## 第一张 · 翻山越岭而来

> 你揣着一张赌石邀帖，一路翻山越岭走到这曲措乡地界。没有姓氏可报，没有师门可攀。

```
masterpiece, best quality, highly detailed anime illustration, cinematic wide shot,
Chinese wuxia landscape painting fused with anime rendering,

SCENE: a lone traveler seen from far behind, small against an enormous Tibetan
highland landscape, walking a thin dirt trail along a mountain ridge,
plain travelling clothes, a cloth bundle on the back, a spear used as a walking staff,
no face visible, no heroic pose — just someone who has been walking a long time,

LANDSCAPE: snow-capped peaks receding into haze, three rivers converging
in the valley far below, dark pine forest on the slopes, prayer flags strung
between two rocks at the trailside, weathered and faded,
late afternoon light, long shadows, cold air with visible breath,

COMPOSITION: wide 16:9 cinematic framing, traveler placed off-center in the
lower third and kept well away from all edges, vast empty sky in the upper half,
bottom-center kept dark and uncluttered (the caption box sits there),
muted palette: slate blue, dusty ochre, pine green, with one small warm accent
(the traveler's clothing),

MOOD: solitary, small, unimportant. The land is huge and does not care that
you are walking through it.
```

**关键**：不要露脸、不要英雄姿态。"没有姓氏可报，没有师门可攀"要靠"背影很小、山很大"来表达。

## 第二张 · 那张邀帖

> 帖上写着：XXX 启。落款一个「温」字——天都镇玉器轩，三日一开石，路远，来不来随意。

```
masterpiece, best quality, highly detailed anime illustration, cinematic close-up,
Chinese wuxia still-life, painterly,

SCENE: a hand-written invitation card held in one hand, seen over the shoulder
at a slight angle, the rest of the frame is the blurred highland trail beyond,
the card is worn: edges frayed and fuzzy from being carried a long way,
one corner soft and rounded, a faint crease across the middle, a thumb-smudge,
brush-written vertical Chinese characters (do not render legible text — suggest
calligraphy with confident brushstrokes rather than spelling anything out),
a single red seal impression near the bottom, small and precise,

LIGHTING: low afternoon sun raking across the paper so the fibre texture shows,
the card is the brightest thing in frame, everything else falls into shadow,

COMPOSITION: wide 16:9 cinematic framing, card occupying the left-of-center
area, kept clear of all four edges,
bottom-center kept dark and uncluttered (the caption box sits there),
shallow depth of field — card sharp, background dissolved,
palette: aged paper cream, cinnabar red seal, cold blue-grey background,

MOOD: this small piece of paper is the only reason you are here.
```

**关键**：要的是"看得出是毛笔字"而**不是真能读出字**。模型写汉字基本都是乱码，
反而毁掉整张——所以负向里 `text, letters, gibberish` 是认真的。

## 负向（两张通用）

```
lowres, bad anatomy, worst quality, watermark, signature, blurry,
text, letters, gibberish characters, garbled writing,
modern clothing, photorealistic, 3d render,
character face visible, front-facing portrait, heroic pose, dynamic action,
crowded composition, subject touching frame edge, vertical composition, portrait orientation
```
